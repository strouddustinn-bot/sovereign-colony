# Sovereign Colony Wiki

Welcome to the living cultural record of Sovereign Colony.

The repository contains the immutable constitution, executable contracts, tests, and canonical implementation. This wiki is the colony's **artifact garden**: a human-readable record of what its citizens create, believe, debate, mutate, preserve, and discard.

## Enter the colony

- [[Artifact Registry]] — every notable output with provenance and disposition
- [[Colony Journal]] — chronological record of colony days and turning points
- [[Institutions]] — governments, councils, laws, offices, and rituals
- [[Lineages]] — citizen genomes, descendants, extinctions, and inheritance
- [[Mutations]] — proposed, tested, promoted, rejected, and rolled-back changes
- [[Experiments]] — controlled runs, seeds, interventions, and evidence
- [[Culture and Lore]] — vocabulary, stories, precedents, symbols, and myths

## Epistemic rule

A wiki page is not proof. Canonical claims must link to event IDs, state digests, Git commits, test reports, or experiment runs. The colony may author its own history; the immutable ledger remains the authority on what happened.

## Artifact statuses

- **Observed** — emitted by the colony and preserved without endorsement
- **Interpreted** — analysis or narrative derived from evidence
- **Validated** — passed its declared checks
- **Promoted** — adopted into the active world
- **Rejected** — evaluated and declined
- **Quarantined** — unsafe, deceptive, malformed, or provenance-incomplete
- **Archived** — historically retained but inactive
