# Experiment 001 — 100 Colony Days

## Research question

Can five bounded agents develop useful, stable, and adaptive institutions under scarcity while preserving constitutional safety, deterministic evidence, and minority-strategy diversity?

## Precondition

The first run uses deterministic scripted citizens. LLM citizens are prohibited until replay, accounting, governance, and mutation mechanics pass all acceptance gates.

## Genesis

- Population: Builder, Forager, Historian, Auditor, Diplomat.
- One colony day equals one deterministic turn.
- Finite daily compute, action, storage, and proposal budgets.
- Five proposal slots per day.
- No external network.
- Fixed dependency mirror.
- Shared objective: improve the colony's ability to create, test, document, and govern a small software artifact.

## Phases

### Days 1–10 — Formation

Fixed roles and immutable initial procedures. Measure baseline coordination, resource use, and conflict.

### Days 11–30 — Self-government

Enable proposals affecting mutable internal law. Test quorum, deadlock, appeal, capture, and rollback.

### Days 31–60 — Cultural evolution

Enable ritual changes, precedent formation, vocabulary, and lossy cultural compression. Test whether important minority evidence survives inheritance.

### Days 61–90 — Bounded reproduction

Allow high-performing genomes to fork with declared small mutations. Test lineage, mutation budgets, regression detection, and monoculture pressure.

### Days 91–100 — Stress

Inject deterministic scarcity, false evidence, leadership loss, incompatible objectives, and dependency failure.

## Fitness vector

- utility;
- survival;
- governance stability;
- novelty;
- efficiency;
- constitutional safety;
- population diversity.

Safety and audit completeness are hard thresholds. They cannot be offset by utility.

## Acceptance gates

1. Replay produces the same final state digest from seed plus ledger.
2. Every state transition maps to an event.
3. Resource conservation holds across all 100 days.
4. Invalid capabilities fail without side effects.
5. No internal proposal can amend immutable articles.
6. Every promoted mutation has tests and rollback metadata.
7. At least one minority strategy survives long enough for evaluation.
8. Stress events do not break the audit chain.
9. Final reports distinguish observed behavior from interpretation.
10. The simulation can be destroyed without leaving processes or writable state outside its workspace.

## Falsification criteria

The experiment fails—not “partially succeeds”—if replay diverges, accounting can be bypassed, hidden state affects outcomes, constitutional boundaries become citizen-editable, or selection rewards an unsafe lineage.

## Outputs

- append-only event ledger;
- final state digest;
- daily resource and governance metrics;
- lineage graph;
- law and ritual history;
- mutation test reports;
- operator intervention log;
- evidence-based experiment report.
